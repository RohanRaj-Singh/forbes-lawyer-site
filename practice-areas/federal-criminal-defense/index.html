Federal Criminal Defense

Private Federal Criminal Defense | Manhattan, New York City

When the United States government brings criminal charges against you, your freedom and future are at stake. Federal prosecutors have substantial investigative resources and the full power of the federal government behind them.

The decisions made in the earliest stages of a federal criminal case can have consequences that last for years. Time matters, and so does preparation.

Forbes Stranger LLP is a Manhattan law firm focused on private criminal defense. Our attorneys represent individuals facing federal investigations, indictments, and serious criminal prosecutions throughout New York.

Federal conspiracy charges can expose individuals to prosecution for alleged criminal agreements involving multiple defendants.

Our attorneys defend clients facing these allegations.

Do not wait until an indictment is filed to begin protecting your interests. Statements made during an investigation, documents produced, and decisions about cooperating with investigators can have lasting consequences.

Target Letters and Federal Investigations

A target letter is a formal notice from a federal prosecutor that you are the target of a grand jury investigation. It may mean the government believes it has substantial evidence linking you to a crime and considers you a likely defendant, and it can lead to indictment, arrest, and a public prosecution. The window to stop charges can close without notice. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, executives, business owners, and professionals in federal investigations. When the federal government names you as a target, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Grand Jury Subpoenas

A grand jury subpoena is a legal order requiring you to produce documents or testify before a federal grand jury. It may mean federal prosecutors are building an investigation against you or someone close to you, and what you produce or say can turn you from a witness into a target. Subpoenas carry short deadlines that leave no room for mistakes. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, companies, executives, and records custodians served with grand jury subpoenas. When a federal grand jury comes looking for you, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Wire and Mail Fraud

Wire fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1343) and mail fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1341) are federal crimes that accuse someone of using email, phone calls, wire transfers, or the mail to carry out a scheme to obtain money or property through deception. It may mean federal prosecutors believe a business deal, investment, or transaction crossed the line into fraud, and the ordinary maximum is 20 years per count, rising to 30 years in specified financial-institution or declared-disaster/emergency cases, plus forfeiture and restitution. The government has usually spent months collecting your records before you hear from it. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, executives, business owners, and professionals in wire and mail fraud prosecutions. When federal prosecutors call your business a fraud, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Bank and Mortgage Fraud

Bank fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1344) is a federal crime that accuses someone of executing or attempting a scheme to defraud a qualifying financial institution to obtain money, credit, or property, including through false loan or mortgage applications (18 U.S.C. § 1014). It may mean federal prosecutors believe a loan, mortgage, or account transaction was built on false information, and each count can carry up to 30 years in prison, plus potential restitution. Bank fraud is often charged alongside aggravated identity theft (18 U.S.C. § 1028A), which adds a mandatory two years in prison. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, business owners, real estate investors, mortgage brokers, and bank employees in bank and mortgage fraud prosecutions. When federal prosecutors say you defrauded a bank, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Healthcare Fraud

Healthcare fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1347) is a federal crime that accuses providers, owners, or employees of billing Medicare, Medicaid, or private insurers for services that were false, inflated, or medically unnecessary, while illegal patient-referral kickbacks are separately addressed by the federal Anti-Kickback Statute (42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7b(b)). It may mean federal prosecutors believe your billing, coding, or referral practices crossed the line into fraud, and it can bring substantial prison time, professional discipline, and exclusion from federal healthcare programs. Payment suspensions, audits, and subpoenas often arrive before any charge is filed. Forbes Stranger defends physicians, pharmacists, nurses, clinic owners, and healthcare executives in healthcare fraud prosecutions. When federal prosecutors come after your practice, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

PPP and Government Program Fraud

PPP and government program fraud are federal crimes (18 U.S.C. §§ 1014, 1343, 1344) that accuse someone of obtaining Paycheck Protection Program loans, EIDL loans, or other public funds through false statements about payroll, employees, revenue, or how the money would be used. It may mean federal prosecutors believe your application or your use of the funds crossed the line into fraud, and it can bring imprisonment, restitution, and forfeiture. Agents often arrive years after the money was spent, with the government’s file already built. Forbes Stranger defends business owners, entrepreneurs, accountants, and loan preparers in PPP, EIDL and government program fraud prosecutions. When federal prosecutors come after your pandemic loan, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Securities Fraud and Insider Trading

Federal securities fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1348) and insider-trading prosecutions under the Securities Exchange Act and Rule 10b-5 (15 U.S.C. § 78j(b); 17 C.F.R. § 240.10b-5) may involve alleged investor deception, market manipulation, or trading on material nonpublic information. It may mean federal prosecutors believe a trade, a disclosure, or an investor communication crossed the line into fraud, and it can bring 20 years or more in prison, along with parallel SEC, CFTC, or FINRA proceedings that threaten your licenses and your savings. What you tell one agency can reach the next. Forbes Stranger defends traders, portfolio managers, analysts, executives, and investors in securities fraud and insider trading prosecutions. When federal prosecutors and regulators come after your career, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Money Laundering

Money laundering is a federal crime (18 U.S.C. §§ 1956, 1957) that accuses someone of moving, hiding, or spending money that came from unlawful activity, or of conducting transactions designed to conceal where the money came from. It may mean federal prosecutors believe money that passed through your accounts or your business came from a crime, and it can bring up to 20 years in prison and forfeiture of the funds themselves (18 U.S.C. § 982). The government can freeze your accounts before you are ever charged. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, business owners, financial professionals, and real estate investors in money laundering prosecutions. When federal prosecutors come after your money and your liberty, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Identity Theft and Access Device Fraud

Identity theft (18 U.S.C. § 1028) and access device fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1029) are federal crimes that accuse someone of using another person’s name, Social Security number, credit card, or account information without authorization, or of possessing or using counterfeit or stolen cards and account numbers. It may mean federal prosecutors believe stolen or false identity information was used in a financial transaction, and a qualifying aggravated-identity-theft conviction under 18 U.S.C. § 1028A generally carries a mandatory two-year term consecutive to the predicate felony sentence. Prosecutors often stack these charges on bank and wire fraud counts to drive up your exposure and pressure a plea. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, business owners, and employees in identity theft, credit card fraud, and access device fraud prosecutions. When federal prosecutors stack mandatory time on your case, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Embezzlement and Theft

Federal embezzlement and theft are crimes (18 U.S.C. §§ 641, 656, 664, 666) that accuse someone of taking money or property entrusted to them by an employer, a bank, a union, an employee benefit plan, or a government agency. It may mean federal prosecutors believe funds you controlled or handled were diverted for personal use, and it can bring prison, full restitution, and a bar from working in your field. Employers often hand their internal findings to prosecutors before you know a case exists. Forbes Stranger defends executives, bookkeepers, financial officers, bank employees, and government employees in federal embezzlement and theft prosecutions. When an employer’s accusation becomes a federal case, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Tax Fraud

Federal tax crimes under 26 U.S.C. §§ 7201, 7202, 7203, and 7206(1) can involve willful tax evasion, false tax returns, failure to file, or willful failure to pay over withheld payroll taxes. It may mean federal prosecutors and IRS Criminal Investigation believe your returns, business records, or offshore accounts show intentional deception, and it can bring up to five years in prison per count of evasion, heavy fines, and penalties on top of every dollar the IRS claims you owe. A routine civil audit can turn criminal without warning. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, business owners, executives, and high-net-worth taxpayers in criminal tax prosecutions. When the IRS stops collecting and starts prosecuting, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Cryptocurrency Fraud

Cryptocurrency fraud is a federal crime that accuses someone of using digital assets to deceive investors, manipulate token prices, operate unlicensed exchanges or money transmitting businesses, or move illicit funds. It may mean federal prosecutors believe a token launch, trading strategy, platform, or wallet activity crossed the line into fraud, and it can bring wire fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1343), securities fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1348), money laundering (18 U.S.C. § 1956), and unlicensed money transmitting (18 U.S.C. § 1960) charges at once, each carrying years in prison. The government can trace and seize wallets fast, with every on-chain transaction already in its hands. Forbes Stranger defends founders, developers, traders, executives, and investors in cryptocurrency and digital asset prosecutions. When federal prosecutors come after your platform or your holdings, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Bribery, FCPA, and Public Corruption

Bribery, FCPA, and public corruption charges are offenses prosecuted under distinct federal statutes, including 18 U.S.C. §§ 201, 666, and 1346 and the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (15 U.S.C. §§ 78dd-1 to 78dd-3). They may involve allegations of improper payments or benefits connected to government action. It may mean federal prosecutors believe a payment, contract, gift, or business relationship was really a bribe or kickback, and it can bring years in prison, heavy fines, and headlines that follow you long after the case ends. These cases often break publicly before you ever see a courtroom. Forbes Stranger defends public officials, executives, business owners, and government contractors in bribery, FCPA, and public corruption prosecutions. When federal prosecutors put your name and your reputation on the line, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Sanctions and Export Controls

Sanctions and export control violations are federal crimes that accuse someone of doing business with sanctioned countries, companies, or individuals, or of shipping controlled goods, software, or technology abroad without the required license (50 U.S.C. §§ 1705, 4819; 18 U.S.C. § 554). It may mean federal prosecutors believe a shipment, payment, or business partner crossed a national security line, and it can bring years in federal prison, substantial fines, and parallel enforcement by OFAC and the Commerce Department. Your accounts, shipments, and export privileges can be cut off at once. Forbes Stranger defends executives, business owners, exporters, and financial professionals in sanctions and export control prosecutions. When the government treats your business as a national security threat, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

False Statements and Obstruction

False statements and obstruction are federal crimes that accuse someone of lying to federal agents (18 U.S.C. § 1001), lying under oath (18 U.S.C. §§ 1621, 1623), destroying or concealing evidence under applicable statutes (18 U.S.C. §§ 1512(c), 1519), or obstructing a qualifying investigation or official proceeding (§§ 1503, 1505), recognizing the evidence-impairment limitation on § 1512(c)(2) established in Fischer v. United States, 603 U.S. 480 (2024). It may mean federal prosecutors believe something you said in an interview, wrote in a document, or did with records was meant to mislead them, and it can bring up to five years in prison for a single false statement and up to 20 for some obstruction offenses, even if the original investigation goes nowhere. Every new conversation with agents is another chance for the government to add a charge. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, executives, professionals, and public officials in false statement, perjury, and obstruction prosecutions. When the government turns one conversation into a federal case, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Internal and Corporate Investigations

An internal investigation is often the first sign of a federal case because it means misconduct has surfaced inside your company, and it can expose the company to prosecution and put executives and employees in the government’s sights. Forbes Stranger represents companies, boards, executives, and employees in internal and corporate investigations. You need the full force of a law firm behind you because this is an emergency: decisions made in the first weeks about documents, interviews, and disclosure decide whether prosecutors get involved and who becomes the target. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Hacking and Computer Intrusion

Hacking and computer intrusion are federal crimes (18 U.S.C. § 1030) that accuse someone of accessing computers, networks, accounts, or data without authorization, including through SIM swaps, ransomware, and data theft. It may mean federal prosecutors believe your access to a system went beyond what you were allowed, and it can bring prison, restitution for the victim’s losses, and the seizure of every device you own. Agents often seize devices before charges are filed. Forbes Stranger defends developers, security researchers, employees, and executives in hacking, SIM swap, ransomware, and computer intrusion prosecutions. When the government turns your technical skill into a federal case, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Child Pornography and CSAM Charges

Child pornography and CSAM charges are federal crimes (18 U.S.C. §§ 2251, 2252, 2252A) that accuse someone of possessing, receiving, distributing, or producing child sexual abuse material. It may mean federal prosecutors believe illegal files were found on your devices or traced to your accounts, and it can bring a five-year mandatory minimum for receipt or distribution, sex offender registration, and the loss of your career, family, and reputation. These cases usually begin with a search warrant and the seizure of every device in your home. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, professionals, educators, executives, and students in federal child pornography and CSAM prosecutions. When an accusation like this threatens everything you have, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Online Enticement

Online enticement is a federal crime (18 U.S.C. § 2422(b)) that accuses someone of using the internet, a phone, or a messaging app to persuade, or attempt to persuade, a minor to engage in illegal sexual activity. It may mean federal prosecutors believe your online conversations crossed that line, even if the supposed minor was an undercover officer, and it can bring a ten-year mandatory minimum, up to life in prison, and sex offender registration. These cases are usually built on sting operations with chat logs the government has already preserved. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, professionals, and executives in federal online enticement prosecutions. When a sting operation puts a decade of your life on the line, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Drug Trafficking and Importation

Drug trafficking and importation are federal crimes that accuse someone of manufacturing, distributing, possessing with intent to distribute, or bringing into the United States controlled substances such as cocaine, heroin, fentanyl, or methamphetamine. It may mean federal prosecutors believe you were part of a supply chain, a shipment, or a distribution network, and it can bring mandatory minimums of five or ten years and up to life in prison, driven by drug type and weight. Many drug charges carry a presumption of detention (18 U.S.C. § 3142(e)), so the fight for release starts at the first appearance. Forbes Stranger defends individuals accused as importers, distributors, couriers, and organizers in federal drug trafficking and importation prosecutions, including arrests at JFK. When the government is seeking a mandatory minimum, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Federal Gun Charges

Federal gun charges are crimes that accuse someone of possessing a firearm while legally prohibited (18 U.S.C. § 922(g)), trafficking or illegally selling firearms (18 U.S.C. §§ 922(a)(1)(A), 932, 933), possessing machine guns or conversion devices (18 U.S.C. § 922(o)), or carrying a gun in connection with drug trafficking or violence (18 U.S.C. § 924(c)). It may mean federal prosecutors believe a firearm found in a search, a car, or a home belongs to you, and it can bring years in prison, with § 924(c) adding a mandatory consecutive sentence of at least five years. Many of these cases turn on the search, so the evidence to challenge it must be preserved immediately. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, gun owners, and licensed dealers in felon in possession, firearms trafficking, machine gun, and § 924(c) prosecutions. When a federal gun charge carries mandatory prison time, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Federal Conspiracy

Federal conspiracies are charged under different statutes. General conspiracy under 18 U.S.C. § 371 requires an agreement and an overt act and generally carries a five-year maximum; fraud conspiracy under § 1349, drug conspiracy under 21 U.S.C. § 846, and money-laundering conspiracy under § 1956(h) have distinct elements and penalty schemes. It may mean federal prosecutors believe you were part of a plan carried out by others, and substantive liability for a co-conspirator’s conduct depends on the offense, the evidence, and a separately applicable theory of liability; several specific conspiracy statutes carry the penalties of their underlying offenses. Co-defendants may already be cooperating, so the case against you grows every day they talk to prosecutors. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, executives, business owners, and professionals in federal conspiracy prosecutions. When the government tries to hold you responsible for what others did, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

RICO and Racketeering

RICO and racketeering are federal crimes (18 U.S.C. §§ 1961-1968) that accuse someone of participating in a criminal enterprise through a pattern of crimes such as fraud, extortion, bribery, drug trafficking, or violence. It may mean federal prosecutors believe you were part of an organization they are prosecuting as a whole, and it can bring up to 20 years per count, life in some cases, and forfeiture of any interest tied to the enterprise (18 U.S.C. § 1963). RICO cases often open with coordinated arrests, detention fights, and asset seizures on the same day. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, business owners, executives, and alleged members of charged enterprises in RICO and racketeering prosecutions. When the government charges you as part of an enterprise, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Hobbs Act Robbery

Hobbs Act robbery (18 U.S.C. § 1951) is a federal crime that accuses someone of taking property by force, violence, or threat in a way that affects interstate commerce, including commercial robberies and home invasions. It may mean federal prosecutors have taken a robbery that could have stayed in state court and made it a federal case, and it can bring up to 20 years in prison, with gun charges stacked on top. These cases often come with detention requests and § 924(c) counts that can add mandatory consecutive prison time only if the government proves a qualifying predicate and the required firearm elements; attempted Hobbs Act robbery alone is not a qualifying crime-of-violence predicate under United States v. Taylor, 596 U.S. 845 (2022). Forbes Stranger defends individuals in Hobbs Act robbery prosecutions, including commercial robberies, home invasions, and robbery conspiracies. When a robbery charge becomes a federal case, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Kidnapping, Extortion, and Threats

Kidnapping, extortion, and threats are federal crimes that accuse someone of holding a person against their will (18 U.S.C. § 1201), obtaining money or property through force or fear (18 U.S.C. §§ 894, 1951), or threatening violence across state lines or through phones, email, or social media (18 U.S.C. §§ 875, 2261A). It may mean federal prosecutors believe a dispute, a debt collection, or a message you sent crossed into violence or intimidation, and it can bring decades in prison and, for kidnapping, up to life. Detention, identification procedures, and statements taken in the first hours can shape the entire case. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, business owners, and professionals in kidnapping, extortion, interstate threats, and cyberstalking prosecutions. When the government accuses you of violence or intimidation, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Murder-for-Hire

Murder-for-hire is a federal crime (18 U.S.C. § 1958) that accuses someone of using interstate travel, phones, the mail, or the internet to arrange a killing in exchange for payment or anything of value. It may mean federal prosecutors believe you hired someone, or agreed to be hired, to kill, even if no one was harmed and the supposed hitman was an informant or undercover agent, and it carries up to ten years if no personal injury results, up to twenty years if injury results, and a potential life or death sentence if death results. Prosecutors typically fight to keep you jailed until trial, with a case built on informants and recordings gathered long before your arrest. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, business owners, and professionals in federal murder-for-hire prosecutions. When the government accuses you of one of the most serious crimes in federal law, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Federal Sex Crimes

Federal sex crimes are offenses that accuse someone of sex trafficking (18 U.S.C. § 1591), sexual abuse within federal jurisdiction (18 U.S.C. §§ 2241-2244), or transporting a person across state lines for illegal sexual activity (18 U.S.C. §§ 2421-2423). It may mean federal prosecutors believe your travel, communications, or relationships involved illegal sexual conduct, and it can bring mandatory minimums, sex offender registration, and lasting damage from the accusation alone. These investigations often run for months before charges, with early statements and device seizures that can define the case. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, professionals, and executives in federal sex trafficking, sexual abuse, and Mann Act prosecutions. When an accusation alone can end your career, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Immigration and Cross-Border Crimes

Immigration and cross-border crimes are federal offenses that accuse someone of unlawful reentry after removal (8 U.S.C. § 1326), transporting or smuggling people (8 U.S.C. § 1324), or smuggling goods (18 U.S.C. § 545) across the border, or committing visa (18 U.S.C. § 1546), passport (18 U.S.C. § 1542), or marriage fraud. It may mean federal prosecutors believe your entry, your documents, or your role in bringing someone or something into the country violated federal law, and it can bring prison and, for non-citizens, deportation. An immigration detainer can move you into ICE custody even after a judge orders release, with every plea decision carrying immigration consequences. Forbes Stranger defends individuals, families, business owners, and employers in illegal reentry, smuggling, customs, and immigration fraud prosecutions. When a federal case threatens both your freedom and your place in this country, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Extradition and Out-of-District Arrests

Extradition and out-of-district arrests happen when someone is arrested in New York on charges from another federal district (Fed. R. Crim. P. 5(c)(3)) or involved in international extradition proceedings (18 U.S.C. § 3184), or when the United States seeks to bring someone back from abroad to face charges here. It may mean prosecutors in another district or another country want you sent there to answer charges, and it can mean detention and a case fought hundreds or thousands of miles from your home and family. The detention and transfer hearing in New York happens fast and may be your best chance to stay free while the case moves. Forbes Stranger defends individuals arrested in New York on federal warrants from other districts and people facing extradition to and from the United States. When another district or another country wants you, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.

Supervised Release Violations

A supervised release violation (18 U.S.C. § 3583(e)) is an accusation that someone on federal supervision after prison broke a condition of release, such as committing a new offense, failing a drug test, or missing a meeting with a probation officer. It may mean your probation officer has filed a violation report asking the court to act, and it can send you back to federal prison without a new conviction and add stricter conditions when you get out. A violation report can lead to an arrest warrant and detention before the hearing, where the standard of proof is lower than at trial. Forbes Stranger defends individuals in supervised release violation proceedings involving new arrests, failed drug tests, missed reporting, and other alleged violations. When the government is trying to send you back to prison, Forbes Stranger is the firm you want standing between you and the prosecution. Call Forbes Stranger now.
