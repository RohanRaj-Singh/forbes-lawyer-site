Sex Crimes & SORA

Sexual offense and registration defense.

Rape, sexual abuse, predatory sexual assault, child pornography, online sting arrests, SORA hearings, registration, and sex offender civil commitment.

A sex crime accusation can carry mandatory minimums, sex offender registration, and lasting damage from the accusation alone — even before conviction. These cases often turn on consent, credibility, and digital evidence that must be preserved immediately.

Rape and sexual assault

The firm defends first-degree, second-degree, and third-degree rape, aggravated sexual abuse, sexual abuse, sexual misconduct, forcible touching, and predatory sexual assault prosecutions throughout New York City.

Child sexual abuse and CSAM

Course of sexual conduct, child pornography, online sting arrests, luring charges, and endangering the welfare of a child cases require forensic examination of interviews, chat logs, and device evidence.

SORA and registration

SORA risk level hearings, modification petitions, failure to register, relocation to New York as a registrant, Article 10 civil commitment, and SARA housing restrictions — the firm represents registrants at every stage.

Online sting operations

Disseminating indecent material to minors and luring charges built on undercover chat operations require careful examination of entrapment, full chat history, and how the conversation began.
