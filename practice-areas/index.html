Practice Areas

Counsel at every stage.

What we do

The matters we are retained to defend.

Private criminal defense with the attention of the named partners and the resources of an entire firm. Four areas of practice, one discipline: the case is built from the first conversation as though it will be tried.

01

Federal
Criminal Defense

Defense of federal investigations and prosecutions in the Southern and Eastern Districts of New York — from target letters and grand jury subpoenas through indictment, trial, and sentencing.

 02

White-Collar Crime &
Government Investigations

Fraud, embezzlement, money laundering, tax, and corruption matters; representation before the DOJ, SEC, Manhattan DA, and New York Attorney General.

 03

Criminal Investigations,
Grand Juries & Pre-Charge
Defense

Representation from first agent contact through charging decisions — stopping charges before they are filed, grand jury proceedings, and pre-indictment strategy.

 04

Gun & Weapons
Charges

Criminal possession of a weapon, firearms trafficking, ghost guns, assault weapons, and CPW charges across all five boroughs — including JFK and LaGuardia airport cases.

 05

Sex Crimes & SORA

Rape, sexual abuse, predatory sexual assault, child pornography, online sting arrests, SORA hearings, registration, and sex offender civil commitment.

 06

Domestic Violence &
Orders of Protection

Domestic violence assault charges, orders of protection, and related family-court and criminal proceedings throughout New York City.

 07

Assault & Violent
Crimes

Assault, robbery, burglary, theft, and violent felony defense in New York's state courts from arraignment through trial.

 08

Murder, Manslaughter &
Attempted Murder

Homicide and attempted homicide defense — jury trials, suppression hearings, and post-conviction relief in state and federal court.

 09

Robbery, Burglary &
Theft Crimes

Grand larceny, embezzlement, robbery, burglary, and theft prosecutions including Hobbs Act robbery and shoplifting-related felony charges.

 10

Drug Crimes

Federal and state drug trafficking, narcotics distribution, possession with intent, and conspiracy prosecutions including arrests at JFK.

 11

DWI & Vehicular
Crimes

Driving while intoxicated and vehicular crime defense throughout New York City and New York State.

 12

Conspiracy, RICO &
Enterprise Crime

Federal conspiracy, RICO racketeering, and enterprise crime prosecutions including coordinated arrests and asset seizure cases.

 13

Sentencing & Post-Conviction
Relief

Criminal appeals, motions to vacate convictions, sentencing challenges, and post-conviction relief in New York's appellate courts and the Second Circuit.

 14

Victim Representation

Building criminal referrals for fraud victims and protecting rights to restitution as the case moves forward — fraud, embezzlement, and investment schemes.

 15

Trial

State and federal jury trials where the courtroom is the only acceptable forum — tried with full preparation from the first conversation.
The discipline of preparation is the only privilege a defendant truly owns. Everything else is borrowed.

How it begins

Engagement, plainly.

01 / First conversation

A call, in confidence

Inquiries are reviewed personally by one of the named partners. Initial consultations are confidential and, where appropriate, conducted off-site.

02 / Assessment

Exposure, forum, timing

We assess the charges or the investigation, the forum in which the matter sits, and what has to happen first — before advising on representation.

03 / Representation

Prepared from the start

Where the firm is retained, the case is built for trial from the beginning, with both named partners involved in strategy.
