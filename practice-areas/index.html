NYC Criminal Defense Lawyers

Practice Areas

New York State Crimes

Forbes Stranger LLP defends individuals accused of crimes under New York law, from misdemeanor charges and first arrests to serious and violent felony prosecutions. Based in Manhattan, our New York criminal defense attorneys represent clients throughout New York City and across New York State at every stage of a criminal case, including investigation, arrest, arraignment, grand jury proceedings, indictment, motion practice, hearings, trial, and sentencing.

A charge under the New York Penal Law is only the beginning of the case. The prosecution must still prove every element of the offense with admissible evidence.

New York Federal Criminal Defense Lawyers

When the United States government brings criminal charges against you, your freedom and future are at stake. Federal prosecutors have substantial investigative resources and the full power of the federal government behind them.

The decisions made in the earliest stages of a federal criminal case can have consequences that last for years. Time matters, and so does preparation.

Forbes Stranger LLP is a Manhattan law firm focused on private criminal defense. Our attorneys represent individuals facing federal investigations, indictments, and serious criminal prosecutions throughout New York.
