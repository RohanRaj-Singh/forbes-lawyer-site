New York Criminal Defense

State courts. From arraignment through trial.

Felony and misdemeanor defense in New York’s state courts — at the first appearance, in motion practice, and at trial.

A New York arrest moves quickly. Arraignment, bail, and the first charging decisions often arrive before a family has had time to organise a defense. The firm is available at that stage, and the earlier counsel is engaged, the more of the record can still be shaped.

Serious felony allegations

The firm represents clients facing homicide, robbery, assault, sex offenses, weapons and firearms charges, and other serious felony allegations. Cases are investigated independently, with expert consultation where the charges require it, and prepared on the assumption that they will be tried.

The Youth Parts

A significant part of the practice involves defending juveniles and adolescents accused of violent crimes, including in New York’s Youth Parts. That representation addresses both the allegations and their consequences for a young client’s education, family, and future — the parts of the case that outlast the disposition.

Investigation and motions

Discovery is reviewed closely, and where the record supports it the firm litigates suppression and other contested hearings. Challenging government evidence is not a posture reserved for trial; it begins with the first return and continues through motion practice.

At trial

Where trial is the only acceptable forum, the case is tried by the named partners. Jury selection, cross-examination, and the structure of the defense are prepared in advance of the courtroom, not in it.

Victims and families

The firm also represents victims of crime and their families through investigations and prosecutions — advising on the process, communicating with prosecutors, and protecting interests that the criminal justice system does not always account for.
