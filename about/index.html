The firm

Prepared for what matters.

Forbes Stranger LLP is a Manhattan law firm focused on private criminal defense, founded on the belief that serious representation requires preparation, collaboration, and unwavering attention to the client.

The firm represents individuals and institutions through the most consequential moments of their lives — quietly, rigorously, and with the discipline of a firm that prepares every case for the courtroom from the very first conversation.

Our work is selective by design. We accept matters where the outcome materially shapes a client’s future, and we apply the resources of the firm with proportionate seriousness. The firm was founded on the conviction that the best defense is one built early, defended thoroughly, and never reduced to convenience.

We do not advertise our results. We do not stage our presence. Clients come to us through introduction and remain through trust. The work is the proof.

Where trial is inevitable, we are prepared. Where trial can be avoided through skill, leverage, or quiet diplomacy, we pursue that path with equal conviction. Either way, the case is built to win.
