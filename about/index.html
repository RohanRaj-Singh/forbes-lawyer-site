About Forbes Stranger LLP

Private Criminal Defense

Forbes Stranger LLP is a private criminal defense firm founded in Downtown Manhattan by trial attorneys with experience in criminal trials, prosecution, constitutional litigation, and complex financial investigations. We bring the preparation, resources, and sophistication of major litigation to individuals and businesses facing serious criminal charges and government investigations.

Our attorneys defend homicide, robbery, violent assault, firearms, sex crime, financial fraud, embezzlement, and white-collar cases in New York state and federal courts. We represent clients before charges are filed, respond to arrests and search warrants, and litigate cases through grand jury proceedings, contested motions, evidentiary hearings, and jury trials. Our practice also includes emergency arraignments, appeals, post-conviction relief, and representation of crime victims.

We defend our clients' freedom, careers, businesses, and reputations at every stage of the criminal justice process.

11 Broadway. Wall Street. Downtown Manhattan.

Forbes Stranger is headquartered at 11 Broadway, steps from Wall Street in the heart of New York City's Financial District. Downtown Manhattan is a global center of finance, commerce, government, and law, home to major financial institutions, multinational businesses, prosecutors, regulators, and courts.

We established our firm here to meet our clients where they are. Our attorneys represent executives and professionals facing allegations of financial misconduct, businesses under government investigation, and individuals charged with serious state and federal crimes. From complex white-collar investigations to major felony trials, we defend clients in matters involving the institutions and authorities that shape New York City.

Our offices are in Downtown Manhattan. Our practice extends throughout New York.

Manhattan | New York County

From our Financial District headquarters, we represent clients throughout Lower Manhattan, Tribeca, SoHo, Midtown, the Upper East Side, the Upper West Side, Harlem, and Washington Heights. Our practice includes New York County Criminal Court, Supreme Court, and federal criminal proceedings in the Southern District of New York.

Brooklyn | Kings County

We defend clients throughout Downtown Brooklyn, Williamsburg, Bedford-Stuyvesant, Bushwick, Crown Heights, Flatbush, and Coney Island, handling felony indictments, contested motions, hearings, and trials in Kings County Criminal Court and Supreme Court.

Queens | Queens County

Our attorneys represent clients in Astoria, Long Island City, Jackson Heights, Flushing, Forest Hills, Jamaica, and Far Rockaway, including serious felony, firearms, and violent crime prosecutions in Queens County Criminal Court and Supreme Court.

The Bronx | Bronx County

We represent clients throughout Mott Haven, Hunts Point, Fordham, Soundview, Riverdale, and Pelham Bay, handling criminal cases in Bronx County Criminal Court and Supreme Court from arraignment through trial and post-conviction proceedings.

Staten Island | Richmond County

Our practice extends throughout St. George, Stapleton, New Dorp, Great Kills, and Tottenville, representing clients in Richmond County Criminal Court and Supreme Court.

Beyond New York City

We also represent clients in Westchester, Nassau, Suffolk, and other New York counties, as well as federal investigations and prosecutions in the Southern and Eastern Districts of New York.

We communicate directly with clients, maintain transparent billing practices, and explain the decisions affecting their cases.
