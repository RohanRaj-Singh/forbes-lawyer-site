Helena Veronique Stranger

Attorney at Law

Helena Veronique Stranger
Founding Partner

A founding partner whose practice spans serious criminal matters, white-collar investigations, victim representation, and cross-border proceedings.

Admitted to practice in New York and Germany, as well as before the United States District Courts for the Southern and Eastern Districts of New York, Helena brings a perspective shaped by prosecution, criminal defense, financial investigations, and two legal systems.

Two legal systems

Helena has worked on both sides of the criminal justice system. Before practicing in New York, she served with the Munich District Attorney’s Office, gaining firsthand insight into how criminal cases are investigated, charged, and presented by the government. She also completed a two-and-a-half-year judicial clerkship through the Munich Higher Regional Court before entering private criminal practice.

White-collar and financial investigations

Her work then developed into white-collar criminal defense and internal investigations. In Germany, Helena handled matters involving fraud, tax offenses, corporate misconduct, and complex investigations for international clients.

Before entering legal practice, she studied business administration and worked in forensic accounting and investigations at PwC, conducting fraud and financial investigations involving major corporate clients. That experience informs her analysis of the financial records, corporate structures, documentary evidence, and investigative methods central to white-collar cases.

Serious criminal defense in New York

In New York, Helena’s practice has expanded into serious criminal defense, including domestic violence, sex offenses, firearms cases, and other felony matters.

Representation of victims

An important part of Helena’s practice is the representation of victims of crime. She represents individuals navigating the criminal justice system, particularly in sensitive matters involving domestic violence, sexual offenses, and related proceedings. Her experience as a prosecutor and defense attorney gives her insight into how allegations are investigated, how prosecutors evaluate proof, how defense counsel challenges that proof, and how the process affects the person at the center of it.

Education, languages, and memberships

Helena earned a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration and completed her German legal education at Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München. She later earned an LL.M. in International Justice and International Dispute Resolution from Fordham University School of Law, graduating with Latin honors. At Fordham, she co-founded the Fordham International Law Association, further developing the international dimension of her practice.

She is a member of the New York State Bar Association and is fluent in English, German, and Swiss German.

At Forbes Stranger LLP, Helena brings prosecutorial insight, international legal experience, financial-investigations training, and disciplined preparation to serious criminal matters.
