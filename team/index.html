Ryan Alexander Forbes

ATTORNEY AT LAW

Ryan Alexander Forbes is a trial lawyer whose practice focuses on criminal defense, government investigations, and complex litigation. He represents individuals, business owners, and executives in state and federal criminal matters, with an emphasis on violent felonies, financial crimes, firearms offenses, and the defense of juveniles accused of serious crimes.

Ryan has tried criminal cases to verdict, secured acquittals and dismissals, litigated contested motions and hearings, and challenged government evidence. He has represented clients facing homicide, robbery, assault, sex offenses, weapons charges, and other serious felony allegations.

A significant part of Ryan's practice involves defending juveniles and adolescents accused of violent crimes, including in New York's Youth Parts. His representation addresses both the criminal allegations and their consequences for young defendants' education, families, and futures.

Ryan represents individuals and businesses in financial crimes, fraud prosecutions, grand larceny matters, and white-collar criminal investigations. His work includes complex financial allegations, federal criminal exposure, and investigations involving the United States Attorney's Office, the New York State Attorney General, Homeland Security, and other federal law-enforcement agencies. He has handled high-profile and press-covered matters and coordinated defense strategies in investigations spanning multiple jurisdictions.

Ryan has been selected to the Top 40 Under 40 in Criminal Defense by The National Trial Lawyers and recognized by The National Black Lawyers as a Top 40 Under 40 attorney in Criminal Defense. He is a member of both The National Black Lawyers and the National Association of Criminal Defense Lawyers.

Ryan has a background in constitutional and civil rights litigation, including representing plaintiffs in federal actions brought under 42 U.S.C. § 1983. His work has addressed police misconduct, prisoners' rights, and government accountability. He previously worked with Prisoners' Legal Services of New York and The Legal Aid Society and assisted the Albany County Community Police Review Board in developing a disciplinary matrix for police misconduct. This experience informs his criminal defense practice, particularly in cases involving constitutional violations, unlawful searches, and law enforcement overreach.

Ryan also represents victims of crime and their families throughout criminal investigations and prosecutions. He assists clients in navigating investigations, communicating with prosecutors, protecting their interests, and seeking accountability through the criminal justice system.

Ryan earned his Juris Doctor from Albany Law School with a Certificate in Criminal Law. He competed nationally as a member of the Albany Law School Moot Court Travel Team, including in the Dean Jerome Prince Memorial Evidence Competition and the Billings, Exum & Frye National Moot Court Competition. He earned his undergraduate degree from Towson University, where he studied Philosophy, Law and Politics.

Ryan served as a judicial intern to the Honorable Richard Rivera, then Supervising Family Court Judge and Acting Supreme Court Justice in New York's Third Judicial District, where he assisted in reviewing motions and drafting judicial decisions.

At Forbes Stranger LLP, Ryan works closely with clients facing serious criminal charges, drawing on his trial experience and background in constitutional litigation to challenge government evidence, protect their rights, and fight for their futures.

Helena Veronique Stranger

ATTORNEY AT LAW

Helena Veronique Stranger is an experienced criminal defense attorney whose practice spans serious criminal matters, white-collar investigations, victim representation, and cross-border proceedings. Admitted to practice in New York and Germany, as well as before the United States District Courts for the Southern and Eastern Districts of New York, Helena brings a perspective shaped by prosecution, criminal defense, financial investigations, and two legal systems.

Helena has worked on both sides of the criminal justice system. Before practicing in New York, she served with the Munich District Attorney’s Office, gaining firsthand insight into how criminal cases are investigated, charged, and presented by the government. She also completed a two-and-a-half-year judicial clerkship through the Munich Higher Regional Court before entering private criminal practice.

Her work then developed into white-collar criminal defense and internal investigations. In Germany, Helena handled matters involving fraud, tax offenses, corporate misconduct, and complex investigations for international clients. Before entering legal practice, she studied business administration and worked in forensic accounting and investigations at PwC, conducting fraud and financial investigations involving major corporate clients. That experience informs her analysis of the financial records, corporate structures, documentary evidence, and investigative methods central to white-collar cases.

In New York, Helena’s practice has expanded into serious criminal defense, including domestic violence, sex offenses, firearms cases, and other felony matters.

Helena has also secured countless dismissals in criminal matters, particularly violent crimes.

An important part of Helena’s practice is the representation of victims of crime. She represents individuals navigating the criminal justice system, particularly in sensitive matters involving domestic violence, sexual offenses, and related proceedings. Her experience as a prosecutor and defense attorney gives her insight into how allegations are investigated, how prosecutors evaluate proof, how defense counsel challenges that proof, and how the process affects the person at the center of it.

Helena earned a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration and completed her German legal education at Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München. She later earned an LL.M. in International Justice and International Dispute Resolution from Fordham University School of Law, graduating with Latin honors. At Fordham, she co-founded the Fordham International Law Association, further developing the international dimension of her practice.

She is a member of the New York State Bar Association and is fluent in English, German, and Swiss German.

At Forbes Stranger LLP, Helena brings prosecutorial insight, international legal experience, financial-investigations training, and disciplined preparation to serious criminal matters.
