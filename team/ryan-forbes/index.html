Ryan Alexander Forbes

Attorney at Law

Ryan Alexander Forbes
Founding Partner

A founding partner of Forbes Stranger LLP and a trial lawyer whose practice focuses on criminal defense, government investigations, and complex litigation.

Ryan Alexander Forbes represents individuals, business owners, and executives in state and federal criminal matters, with an emphasis on violent felonies, financial crimes, firearms offenses, and the defense of juveniles accused of serious crimes.

Ryan has tried criminal cases to verdict, secured acquittals and dismissals, litigated contested motions and hearings, and challenged government evidence. He has represented clients facing homicide, robbery, assault, sex offenses, weapons charges, and other serious felony allegations.

Juvenile defense

A significant part of Ryan’s practice involves defending juveniles and adolescents accused of violent crimes, including in New York’s Youth Parts. His representation addresses both the criminal allegations and their consequences for young defendants’ education, families, and futures.

Financial crimes and investigations

Ryan represents individuals and businesses in financial crimes, fraud prosecutions, grand larceny matters, and white-collar criminal investigations. His work includes complex financial allegations, federal criminal exposure, and investigations involving the United States Attorney’s Office, the New York State Attorney General, Homeland Security, and other federal law-enforcement agencies. He has handled high-profile and press-covered matters and coordinated defense strategies in investigations spanning multiple jurisdictions.

Constitutional and civil rights

Ryan has a background in constitutional and civil rights litigation, including representing plaintiffs in federal actions brought under 42 U.S.C. § 1983. His work has addressed police misconduct, prisoners’ rights, and government accountability. He previously worked with Prisoners’ Legal Services of New York and The Legal Aid Society and assisted the Albany County Community Police Review Board in developing a disciplinary matrix for police misconduct. This experience informs his criminal defense practice, particularly in cases involving constitutional violations, unlawful searches, and law enforcement overreach.

Representation of victims

Ryan also represents victims of crime and their families throughout criminal investigations and prosecutions. He assists clients in navigating investigations, communicating with prosecutors, protecting their interests, and seeking accountability through the criminal justice system.

Education

Ryan earned his Juris Doctor from Albany Law School with a Certificate in Criminal Law. He competed nationally as a member of the Albany Law School Moot Court Travel Team, including in the Dean Jerome Prince Memorial Evidence Competition and the Billings, Exum & Frye National Moot Court Competition. He earned his undergraduate degree from Towson University, where he studied Philosophy, Law and Politics.

Ryan served as a judicial intern to the Honorable Richard Rivera, then Supervising Family Court Judge and Acting Supreme Court Justice in New York’s Third Judicial District, where he assisted in reviewing motions and drafting judicial decisions.

At Forbes Stranger LLP, Ryan works closely with clients facing serious criminal charges, drawing on his trial experience and background in constitutional litigation to challenge government evidence, protect their rights, and fight for their futures.
