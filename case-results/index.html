A note on results

We do not advertise outcomes.

The firm does not publish a catalogue of verdicts, dismissals, or sentences. That is a deliberate choice rather than an oversight, and it rests on three reasons.

Every case is different

A result in one matter does not predict a result in another. Charges, facts, evidence, the jurisdiction, the judge, and the client’s own history all bear on what happens. A page of favourable outcomes would say more about selection than about what any future client should expect.

Confidentiality outlasts the case

Much of the firm’s work is confidential by nature — investigations that never become public, matters that resolve before charge, and clients for whom the fact of representation is itself sensitive. Describing those matters in a marketing page would trade a client’s privacy for the firm’s promotion.

The record is in the courtroom

Where a matter is litigated, the result is a matter of public record in the court where it was decided. Where a client wishes to discuss the firm’s work directly, we encourage the conversation. We do not ask former clients to provide testimonials, and we do not buy recognition.

Prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome. Any results published by the firm in future will be accurate, verifiable, and accompanied by the disclosures required by the New York Rules of Professional Conduct.
