Case Results

Selected matters

VIOLENT FELONY BURGLARY FIRST DEGREE INDICTMENT — DISMISSED AND SEALED

The prosecution brought a fourteen-count felony indictment carrying potential aggregate exposure of up to 50 years in state prison. The indictment included three counts of Burglary in the First Degree (Penal Law § 140.30), two counts of Criminal Use of a Firearm in the First Degree (Penal Law § 265.09), and related kidnapping, weapons, and assault charges. Following defense motion practice, our attorneys secured the complete dismissal of the indictment.

$200,000 WHITE COLLAR EMBEZZLEMENT ALLEGATIONS — NO CRIMINAL CHARGES FILED

Our attorneys defended an individual facing allegations of nearly $200,000 in misappropriated company funds and potential criminal prosecution. The investigation was closed without any criminal charges being filed.

VIOLENT FELONY ASSAULT FIRST AND SECOND DEGREE CHARGES — DISMISSED AND SEALED

The prosecution charged three violent felonies, including two counts of Assault in the First Degree (Penal Law § 120.10), each carrying up to 25 years in prison, and one count of Assault in the Second Degree (Penal Law § 120.05), carrying up to seven years. Our attorneys secured the dismissal of all charges, and the case was subsequently sealed.

VIOLENT FELONY ATTEMPTED ROBBERY CHARGES — DISMISSED AND SEALED

The prosecution charged two counts of Attempted Robbery in the Second Degree (Penal Law §§ 110.00, 160.10), carrying up to 7 years in state prison, following allegations involving the display of a firearm. Already on probation for a prior gun possession case, the defendant faced additional consequences. Following a contested felony hearing, our attorneys secured the dismissal of all charges after the prosecution failed to meet its burden of proof. The case was subsequently sealed.

VIOLENT FELONY GANG ASSAULT AND ASSAULT FIRST DEGREE CHARGES DISMISSED AND SEALED

The prosecution brought four criminal charges in Queens County, including Gang Assault in the First Degree (Penal Law § 120.07), Assault in the First Degree (Penal Law § 120.10), Assault in the Second Degree (Penal Law § 120.05), and Criminal Possession of a Weapon in the Fourth Degree (Penal Law § 265.01). The charges included two Class B violent felonies, each carrying up to 25 years in state prison. Our attorneys secured the complete dismissal of all four charges.

ASSAULT CHARGES — DISMISSED AND SEALED

The prosecution charged a New York City teacher with Assault in the Third Degree (Penal Law § 120.00), Endangering the Welfare of a Child (Penal Law § 260.10), and Harassment in the Second Degree (Penal Law § 240.26). Despite prosecutors declaring readiness for trial and offering a plea agreement, our attorneys conducted an independent investigation and challenged the evidence. The prosecution ultimately moved to dismiss all charges, and the case was subsequently sealed.

WEAPONS POSSESSION CHARGES — DISMISSED AND SEALED

Following a traffic stop and arrest, the prosecution charged Criminal Possession of a Weapon. Our attorneys secured the complete dismissal of the charges, and the case was subsequently sealed.

FELONY SEXUAL ABUSE INDICTMENT — DISMISSED WITH PREJUDICE AND SEALED

The prosecution brought a three-count indictment charging Sexual Abuse in the First Degree (Penal Law § 130.65), a Class D violent felony carrying up to seven years in state prison, Forcible Touching (Penal Law § 130.52), and Endangering the Welfare of a Child (Penal Law § 260.10). Our attorneys secured the complete dismissal of the indictment with prejudice before trial, and the case was subsequently sealed.

NINE-COUNT FIREARMS TRIAL — FULL ACQUITTAL

The prosecution brought a nine-count indictment involving ghost gun possession and related weapons offenses, including Criminal Possession of a Firearm (Penal Law § 265.01-b) and Attempted Criminal Possession of a Weapon in the Third Degree (Penal Law §§ 110.00, 265.02), each carrying up to four years in state prison. Following a jury trial involving DNA evidence, expert testimony, and law enforcement witnesses, our attorneys secured not-guilty verdicts on every count.
